Hi, I'm Srish! I'm a PhD student and Graduate Researcher in Communications (Rhetoric, Media, and Publics) at Northwestern University in Evanston, IL, USA. I'm an affiliate researcher with the Community Data Science Collective, and the Science in Human Cultures Cluster. I research how people experience belonging and citizenship to their local, national, and global communities while participating in techno-scientific processes and infrastructure. I'm advised by Professors Robert Hariman and Aaron Shaw at Northwestern.  

My work sits at the intersection of rhetoric, science and technology studies (STS), and critical information studies. Through research, public work, and teaching, I attempt to ask (and hopefully, answer) a broad question: _how do we learn from each other?_ 

I also assist and teach classes in media theory and practice, science and climate communications, and persuasive public speaking. 

Outside of research and work, I'm a long-term community organizer in higher education, and I like to cook for my friends and family.

Taught by Professor James Schwoch, this class is offered to undergraduates across Northwestern who want to learn about contemporary public media, policy, and governance of Outer Space. We look at issues such as the vanishing of dark and quiet skies, the growing environmental concerns of space debris, and D2D satellites. 

I provide feedback, grading, and facilitation of mainly oral/groups presentation-based assignments, and close reading responses. Weather permitting, we observe the ISS, Tiangong, BlueWalker 3, and the rest of the Evanston night sky!
